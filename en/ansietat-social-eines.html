Cognitive behavioural therapy

Social anxiety: CBT tools

Three tools to work on the fear of being judged: ranking what you avoid, questioning thoughts and testing them in practice.

A support, not a treatmentThey work better alongside a psychologist. Everything is stored only in your browser. If you feel overwhelmed, stop and see urgent help.

🪜 1. Exposure ladder

List social situations you avoid or find hard, and rate the fear from 0 (none) to 100 (maximum). Start with the low scores and work up gradually. Repeat each situation until the fear goes down.

    Tip: choose a step that gives you about 30-50 fear, not the hardest one. Staying in the situation until the anxiety drops teaches you that you can tolerate it.
