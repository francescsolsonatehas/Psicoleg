Self-help

My plan

Answer five questions about the last two weeks and you will get a proposal of small steps, with links to this website's tools. You can swap any steps that don't suit you.

A guide, not a diagnosisThe plan is only a guide and does not replace a professional. Your answers and plan are stored only in your browser.

📝 The last two weeks

How often has this happened over the last two weeks? There are no right or wrong answers.

1. Have you slept badly: found it hard to fall asleep, woken up often or too early?
2. Have you felt sad or had no desire to do things you used to enjoy?
3. Have you felt nervous, restless or full of worries you can't switch off?
4. Have you felt overwhelmed, with too much on your plate (work, studies, home)?
5. Have you felt lonely or avoided being around other people?
Optional: have you thought that it would be better not to be here, or thought about harming yourself?

🧭 Your plan

Thank you for saying soThinking about harming yourself is more common than it seems, and it can be talked about. Call 024 now (free, 24 hours) or, if you are in immediate danger, 112. You don't have to go through this alone.
