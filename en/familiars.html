CAESEN
For those who support someone

For family members and carers

Supporting someone who is suffering psychologically is not easy. You deserve support too.

What you can say

  • “I’m here and I’m listening.”
  • “That must be really hard. Thank you for telling me.”
  • “You’re not a burden to me.”
  • “Shall we look for help together?”

What it is best to avoid

  • “Cheer up”, “it’s not that bad”, “you have to be strong”.
  • Comparing their suffering with other people’s.
  • Judging, blaming or joking about their condition.
  • Promising to keep secrets if their life is at risk.

How to help in practice

  • Offer to go with them to their first appointment with the doctor or psychologist.
  • Help with basic routines: sleeping, eating, going for a walk.
  • Respect their pace: sometimes help is just being there.
  • Read up with the condition sheets.
If you suspect they are thinking of harming themselvesAsk them directly and calmly. Asking does not increase the risk. If there is immediate danger, don’t leave them alone and call 112. For guidance, 024 also supports relatives.

And how are you?

Caring is also tiring. Make time for yourself, talk to someone you trust and consider family associations, where there are people who have been through similar situations.
