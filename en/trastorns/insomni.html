Sleep

🌙 Insomnia and sleep problems

Difficulty sleeping that affects daily life.

Information, not a diagnosisThis page is for information only. Only a professional can assess your situation.

What it is

Sleep and mental health are closely linked: sleeping badly can worsen mood and anxiety, and vice versa.

Common symptoms

  • Difficulty falling asleep or frequent waking
  • Waking too early
  • Tiredness during the day
  • Irritability and difficulty concentrating

Myths and facts

Myth: You must always sleep 8 hours.
Fact: Needs vary from person to person.
Myth: Pills are the solution.
Fact: For chronic insomnia, therapy is the first-line treatment.

When to see a professional

If insomnia lasts more than three nights a week for over a month.

What treatments do clinical guidelines recommend?

Cognitive behavioural therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is the recommended treatment. Sleep hygiene measures: regular schedules, avoiding screens and caffeine before bed, a dark, cool bedroom.

Tips for family and friends

  • Respect their schedules and rest.
  • Avoid turning sleep into a source of pressure.
