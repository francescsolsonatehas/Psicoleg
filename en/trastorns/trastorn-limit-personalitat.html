Personality

🎭 Borderline personality disorder

Intense emotional instability, in relationships and in one’s self-image.

If you are in danger nowCall 112 or 024 (free, 24 h). You don’t have to face this alone.
Information, not a diagnosisThis page is for information only. Only a professional can assess your situation.

What it is

It is marked by very intense emotions that change quickly, fear of abandonment, unstable relationships and impulsivity. It is a treatable disorder with a good outlook given the right support.

Common symptoms

  • Intense fear of being abandoned
  • Intense, unstable relationships
  • Rapid mood changes
  • Chronic feeling of emptiness
  • Impulsivity or self-harming behaviours
  • Anger that is hard to control

Myths and facts

Myth: They are manipulative people.
Fact: They often feel enormous emotional pain and use the strategies they have.
Myth: They can’t be treated.
Fact: Specific therapies such as DBT have good results.

When to see a professional

If unstable emotions or relationships cause you ongoing distress, or there is self-harm.

What treatments do clinical guidelines recommend?

Specific psychotherapies: dialectical behaviour therapy (DBT), mentalisation-based therapy, among others.

Tips for family and friends

  • Validate their emotions without validating every behaviour.
  • Set clear, kind limits.
  • Look for support for yourself: caring for someone is demanding.
