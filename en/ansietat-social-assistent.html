Experimental · optional

CBT assistant with AI

Practise social conversations, reframe thoughts and prepare behavioural experiments with an AI model that runs on your device.

It is not a therapistIt is an experimental AI and can be wrong, especially in Catalan. It does not diagnose or replace a professional. If you are in crisis, call 024 or 112.

1. Activate the assistant

Nothing is downloaded until you ask. Conversations are not stored or sent anywhere: they stay on the screen and disappear when you close the page.

Where do you want it to run?
For technical reasons, English works betterSmall AI models that run on your device understand and write English (and Spanish) noticeably better than Catalan, where they tend to make mistakes or lose the thread. That is why the assistant chats in English by default. The rest of the website is available in your language.

When you activate it, the library from jsDelivr and the model from Hugging Face will be downloaded (third-party services, which may log your IP address). It is stored in the browser cache.

Start Ollama allowing this website and download a model. In the terminal:

OLLAMA_ORIGINS=https://francescsolsonatehas.github.io ollama serve
ollama pull llama3.2

The website will only talk to your Ollama at localhost; nothing leaves your computer. Some browsers (e.g. Safari) block this connection from an https website.
