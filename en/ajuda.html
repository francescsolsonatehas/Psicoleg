Find help

Phone numbers and services for different moments. If in doubt, call: no one will judge you.

Helplines

Emergencies

Immediate risk to life or physical safety.

112

Suicide prevention line

Free, confidential and open 24 h every day. For anyone thinking of harming themselves and for relatives.

024

ANAR – children and teenagers

Confidential, free help for under-18s, 24 h. Also on 116 111. Relatives and schools: 600 50 51 52.

900 20 20 10

Salut Respon (Catalonia)

Telephone health service 24 h; directs you to health services.

061

Telèfon de l’Esperança (Hope Line)

Emotional support, someone to listen and keep you company (Barcelona).

93 414 48 48

Public healthcare in Catalonia

  • CAP (primary care centre): the family doctor is the first step. They can assess, advise and refer to mental health services.
  • CSMA / CSMIJ: Adult and Child and Youth Mental Health Centres. Access is normally by referral.
  • CAS: Centres for addiction care and follow-up.
  • Hospital emergency departments: if there is serious, immediate risk.

Private care and associations

  • Official College of Psychology of Catalonia: you can check that a psychologist is registered.
  • Salut Mental Catalunya: federation of associations of people with mental health problems and their families.
  • Canal Salut: the Government of Catalonia’s official health portal.
Outside CataloniaIn the rest of Spain, 024 is free and works nationwide. Outside Spain, check the local emergency service.

Phone numbers and services may change. Check them on official websites before relying on a number.
