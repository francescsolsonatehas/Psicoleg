Screening: PHQ-9 and GAD-7

Short questionnaires that measure symptoms of depression and anxiety over the last two weeks.

This is not a diagnosisResults do not replace a clinical assessment. Your answers are not sent anywhere: they are calculated on your device.

The items are an English-language presentation of the PHQ-9 and GAD-7 scales (Pfizer Inc., free to use). They must be interpreted by a professional.
