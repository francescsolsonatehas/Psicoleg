Psychoeducation

Conditions and disorders

Plain-language sheets: what they are, how to recognise them, which myths exist and when to ask for help.

This is not a diagnosisNobody can diagnose themselves by reading a website. If you recognise yourself in a sheet, talk to a professional.

List of conditions

🌊Anxiety and related

Generalised anxiety disorder

Excessive worry that is hard to control, on most days, for months.

💨Anxiety and related

Panic disorder and agoraphobia

Unexpected panic attacks and fear that they will happen again.

🫥Anxiety and related

Social anxiety disorder

Intense fear of being judged or embarrassed in social situations.

🔁Anxiety and related

Obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD)

Intrusive thoughts (obsessions) and rituals (compulsions) that briefly relieve distress.

⚡Trauma and grief

Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)

Lasting reactions after a traumatic experience.

🌧️Mood

Depression

Persistent sadness or loss of interest that affects daily life.

🎢Mood

Bipolar disorder

Alternating episodes of elevated mood (mania/hypomania) and depression.

🍽️Eating

Eating disorders

Anorexia, bulimia and binge-eating disorder: a troubled relationship with food and the body.

🌀Psychosis

Schizophrenia and psychotic disorders

Changes in how reality is perceived: hallucinations, delusions and disorganised thinking.

🎭Personality

Borderline personality disorder

Intense emotional instability, in relationships and in one’s self-image.

⚡Neurodevelopment

ADHD (Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder)

Persistent difficulties with attention, hyperactivity and/or impulsivity.

🧩Neurodevelopment

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD)

A different way of communicating, relating and perceiving the world.

🔗Addictions

Addictions and problematic use

Loss of control over substance use or behaviours (gambling, screens...).

🌙Sleep

Insomnia and sleep problems

Difficulty sleeping that affects daily life.

🕯️Trauma and grief

Grief and loss

A natural process of reacting to a loss, which may need support.

🤝Risk and crisis

Self-harm and suicidal thoughts

If you are thinking of harming yourself right now, you are not alone: ask for help now.

🕷️Anxiety and related

Specific phobias

Intense, out-of-proportion fear of a specific thing or situation (animals, heights, blood, flying...).

🧩Trauma and grief

Complex PTSD and ongoing trauma

Effects of repeated or prolonged trauma, often from childhood: beyond memories, it affects how you see yourself and relate to others.

🩺Anxiety and related

Health anxiety (hypochondria)

Persistent worry about having or being able to develop a serious illness, despite medical checks.

😴Sleep

Other sleep disorders (apnoea, sleepiness, nightmares)

It isn’t all insomnia: apnoea, hypersomnia, narcolepsy, nightmares and parasomnias also affect rest.

🍎Eating

Eating disorders across the lifespan

How eating disorders can show up in childhood, adolescence, adulthood and older age.
