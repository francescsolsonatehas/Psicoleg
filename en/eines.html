Self-help

Tools for everyday life

Short exercises based on well-known techniques. They are a support, not a treatment.

Please noteIf an exercise makes you uncomfortable or feel worse, stop it. If you are in crisis, go to urgent help.

🫁 Guided breathing

Follow the circle. Choose a pattern and breathe through your nose if you can.

Ready?

Press start whenever you are ready.

🌿 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Helps bring you back to the present when anxiety or thoughts overwhelm you.

5 things you see

Look around you and name, quietly or in your head, five things you can see.

💆 Progressive muscle relaxation

Tense each muscle group for about 5 seconds and let go for 10. Do it sitting or lying down.

Ready?

Press start and it will guide you step by step.
